# Marquee

**A beautiful home for your films and TV shows, wherever they are kept.**

Marquee brings together the films and shows on your computer, your phone, Google Drive, OneDrive, Plex and
Jellyfin into one library with posters, plots, cast and ratings, and plays them all, picking up where you left off
on any of your devices. It is inspired by Infuse.

## Your films, from everywhere

- **Folders** on your PC, or on your phone and its SD card.
- **Google Drive** and **OneDrive**: stream straight from the cloud, nothing to download first.
- **Plex** and **Jellyfin / Emby** servers, at home or far away.
- A dot shows which sources are reachable; titles only on a server that is down are set aside until it is back.

## A library that looks after itself

- Every title matched with **TMDB**: posters, backdrops, logos, plots, cast and crew, genres, seasons and episodes.
- **IMDb** ratings next to the year, and **Rotten Tomatoes** and **Metacritic** scores (with a free OMDb key).
- **Home** with Continue watching, Up next, Recently added, Watchlist, Favourites and more; choose and arrange the
  rows yourself.
- **Movies** and **TV Shows** as poster walls or lists, with filters for genre, decade and IMDb rating, and many
  sort orders, remembered until you clear them.
- **Collections** (film series grouped automatically), **actor and director pages**, and a **calendar** of new
  episodes of the shows you follow and release dates of films on your watchlist.
- **Missing episodes** shown greyed out on a show's page.
- **Others**: anything that could not be matched by itself, with likely matches to approve in one tap,
  **Auto-approve** for the sure ones, and search by name, **TMDB link or code, or IMDb code** for the rest.
- **Fix a match** or edit a title, year or plot at any time; tell Marquee when one copy is a different film.
- **Select** several titles at once (right-click on Windows, press and hold on Android) to mark them watched, add
  them to your watchlist or favourites, or hide them.
- **Hide** anything you don't want to see; bring it back from Settings.
- Files that go missing keep their history for 30 days, in case they come back.

## Playback

- Plays almost anything: MKV, MP4, HEVC, HDR, Dolby and DTS audio, and image and styled subtitles (Windows plays
  every format; the Android player is catching up on DTS/TrueHD and styled subtitles).
- **Resume** exactly where you stopped, on any of your devices.
- **Skip intro, recap and credits**, with a button or by itself, and the **next episode** on a countdown.
- **Audio and subtitle choices remembered** per show and film; **subtitles from OpenSubtitles** while you watch, in
  the size and style you like.
- **Seek bar previews** from your Plex and Jellyfin servers (and for files on your PC).
- **Fast starts**: the beginning of a title is read ahead while you look at it, and several connections at once
  speed up far-away servers.
- Speed, chapters, picture size, subtitle and audio timing (Windows), lock, brightness and volume swipes, double-tap
  to skip and picture-in-picture (Android).
- **Cast** to a Chromecast or Google TV.
- **Extras** from your server: behind the scenes, deleted scenes and featurettes.
- Display refresh-rate matching, night mode and clear dialogue sound (Windows).

## Offline

- **Download** a film, an episode or a whole season to watch without a connection, with progress, pause and
  resume; downloads keep going in the background on Android.

## Your devices, in step

- **Google sync** (optional): sources, matches, hidden titles, ratings, what you have watched and your settings
  travel between your PC and phone through your own Google Drive.
- **Trakt**: what you watch is scrobbled, and watched marks, ratings and the watchlist go both ways.
- **Plex and Jellyfin** are told what you watched in Marquee.
- **Backup** your library to a file (Windows).

## Looks and feel

- A dark **liquid-glass** design, made for big posters and backdrops.
- Right-click (Windows) or press and hold (Android) any poster or tile for everything you can do with it.
- Settings in clear categories; rows that scroll with arrows, a swipe or Shift + wheel.

## Installing

**Windows.** Download `Marquee-Setup-<version>.exe` from the
[latest release](https://github.com/samyak3397/marquee-releases/releases/latest) and run it. The installer is not
signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info → Run anyway**. Marquee installs for
your account only and updates itself afterwards.

**Android.** Download [`Marquee.apk`](https://github.com/samyak3397/marquee-releases/releases/latest/download/Marquee.apk)
on your phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or files app the first time,
and later from Marquee itself so it can update.

**Getting started.** Open Settings → Sources and add a folder, Google Drive, OneDrive, Plex or Jellyfin. Matching
titles uses a free [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/settings/api) key, which Marquee asks for in Settings. Online
subtitles (OpenSubtitles), Rotten Tomatoes scores (OMDb) and Trakt use free keys of your own too, all optional.
Signing in with Google may show "Google hasn't verified this app": Marquee is a personal project, choose
**Advanced → Continue**.
